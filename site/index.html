The crew a project needs

Code alone won’t launch a coin. You need people whose strengths complement yours. Pactora is where they find each other, agree on the work before it starts, and get paid for what they actually contribute.

Free to join. No token needed.

The disconnect

Devs build products. Marketers bring supporters. Artists make the brand. They rarely meet.

Most projects stall because the people they need never find them, or join on a handshake and walk away when it gets hard. Pactora puts everyone in one place and writes the deal down first.

01 — The team

Fill the seats you are missing.

A project picks the roles it needs. It doesn’t need every role to start.

02 — How it works

From a skill to a shipped project.

  1. 01

    Show your work

    Build a profile from real samples: code, art, videos, campaigns. Trust comes from evidence, not followers.

  2. 02

    Find a project

    Browse open roles, or post your own project and list the seats you need filled.

  3. 03

    Talk first

    Every application opens a private conversation. Nobody commits until both sides are sure.

  4. 04

    Sign the pact

    Deliverables, deadlines, review and pay are written down and signed by every member’s wallet.

  5. 05

    Build and get paid

    Milestones are reviewed by someone else on the team, and payouts are verified on-chain.

03 — The agreement

Write down the work and the reward.

Who owns the task, when it is due, what finished means, and how that person is paid. A pact is frozen and hashed when it is sent. Any change clears every signature, so nobody signs something they didn’t read.

  • Fixed pay, vesting tokens, an earnings share, or a mix
  • Clear rules for ownership and for leaving the team
  • Pactora never holds your funds
